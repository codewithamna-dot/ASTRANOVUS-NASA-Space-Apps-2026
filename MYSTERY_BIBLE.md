# Mystery bible (spoilers)

## Solution
Mara Rook, the estate engineer, murders Dr. Adrian Vale after he discovers her falsified invoices. The office is bolted from inside, but the bookshelf conceals a service passage to the west stair. The small silver key opens the split-ring service latch, not the main door. Rook takes it and leaves via the service route. The untouched door bolt and dry window sill are genuine observations, not impossible locks.

- 22:03: Vale is alive; the silver key rests on the desk. The temporal echo shows the clock normally.

- 22:12: Detective arrives. Vale is dead, key absent, clock stopped at 22:12, office still bolted and window untouched.

## Suspects
- Voss: denied research credit; claims east laboratory alibi. Familiar with office. Ribbon appears suspicious but gift label dates it to yesterday; it does not prove presence during murder.
- Rook: falsified invoices exposed; claims boiler-room alibi. Knows service routes. Maintenance tools could explain old shelf marks, so scratches alone are insufficient. Fresh route, heel defect and ledger interval contradict the alibi jointly.
- Thorn: rejected prototype sale and financial dispute; claims early departure. Had office access earlier. Receipt is suspicious but departure stamp supports leaving at 21:35.
