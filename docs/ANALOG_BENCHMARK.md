Nearby duplicates (same target, same 2.5 degree cell) are rejected by a test.

## Inclusion criteria

1. A traceable source that states the site was used or proposed as an analog, with a clear objective.
2. A defensible relationship to one of the nine modelled targets; relationship and limitations are written per pair.
3. Not a duplicate of an existing site in the same 2.5 degree cell for the same target.
4. Weak or unverified support makes a site `exploratory` (reported separately, excluded from primary metrics).
5. Hypotheses (team-proposed, no verified analog literature) are never positives.
6. Coordinates are approximate centroids from general geographic knowledge unless stated; they are not taken from the cited papers.
